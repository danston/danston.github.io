# 4Toddler App

At HackZurich in 2014, my colleague and I embraced the challenge of conceptualizing and developing an application within a tight three-day timeframe. Our innovation resulted in a baby-focused app. Recognizing the very young age of our users and their limited screen interaction abilities, we opted for the iPad due to its expansive screen size. We crafted a user-friendly interface tailored to the needs of infants. The app's concept aimed to introduce abstract geometric shapes and colors to the youngest learners. Babies could touch the screen to generate random colorful shapes, which would gradually fade away. Parents had the option to select shapes of varying complexity. Over time, we expanded the app to include additional themed shapes such as autumn, winter, and more.

### Challenges

Crafting a functional prototype within the intense 2.5-day timeframe of non-stop work posed a notable challenge, especially considering the need to ensure its suitability for toddler interaction. Striking the right balance was crucial; colors had to be vibrant enough to captivate young minds while not being overly bright to protect sensitive toddler eyes. Simultaneously, the geometric shapes needed to be clearly visible, striking a harmonious visual balance. Managing the duration of shape visibility added another layer of complexity — it shouldn't vanish too quickly to allow toddlers to engage meaningfully, yet it had to disappear at a pace that maintained a well-covered screen, ensuring an optimal learning experience.
